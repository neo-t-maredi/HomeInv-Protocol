02 / ACCOUNTING BOUNDARIES

Yield earned.
Equity reserved.

01

Each unit has a destination

Investor and treasury allocations round down to the smallest token unit. The remaining units go to the tenant reserve.

02

Previous claims stay recorded

Available yield is the investor’s share of cumulative allocations minus what they have already claimed.

03

A reserve is not a title deed

The prototype records balances. It does not establish property ownership, legal compliance, redemption rights, or a live investment offering.
